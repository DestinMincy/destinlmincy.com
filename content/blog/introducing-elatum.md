If you manage a short-term rental, you already know the grind: guest questions coming in at 10 PM, pricing decisions that feel like a coin flip, and reviews that go unanswered because you forgot. I built ELATUM because I kept hearing the same problem from STR hosts I talked to. The work is never fully done, and most of it is repeatable.

ELATUM is an AI agent built specifically for Airbnb and VRBO hosts. It handles the operational layer so you can focus on the property itself.

## What it does

**Guest communication.** ELATUM reads incoming messages, understands context, and responds the way you would. Check-in instructions, early check-out requests, Wi-Fi passwords, restaurant recommendations: all handled without you touching your phone at midnight. You set the tone and limits; the agent handles the execution.

**Dynamic pricing suggestions.** Pricing short-term rentals well is a moving target. Demand shifts by season, by local event, by competitor availability. ELATUM monitors that landscape and surfaces specific pricing recommendations with the reasoning behind them. You approve or adjust; the agent does not automatically change your rates without your sign-off.

**Review management.** Reviews drive bookings. ELATUM drafts responses to guest reviews so you are not starting from a blank page. For five-star reviews it keeps responses warm and specific. For anything less, it helps you respond professionally without getting defensive.

## Who it helps most

ELATUM is built for hosts managing more than one property. When you have two, three, or five listings, the coordination overhead gets real. Responding to six simultaneous guest conversations while trying to flip a property is not a sustainable system.

Single-property hosts benefit too, especially when you travel or have a full-time job. The agent does not replace you; it keeps things moving when you cannot be the one moving them.
